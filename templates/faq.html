How can we help?

Browse the topics below, or contact our team if you need a hand.

01How do I book a room?

Choose Rooms to compare the Standard, Deluxe and Imperial options, then select “Book this room”. You can also start with Book now and choose a room in the form.

02What details do I need to complete a booking?

The booking form asks for your name, phone number, email address, room choice, arrival date and checkout date. Your room is held briefly while you complete secure payment through Razorpay.

03What amenities are available in the rooms?

Room details include complimentary Wi-Fi, a TV with Chromecast, telephone, tea and coffee facilities, and an en-suite bathroom. Visit the room details for more information about each room.

04Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is included with the room rates shown on our room detail pages. Please check your selected room details when planning your stay.

05How can I ask a question about my stay?

Send a message through our Contact page and our team will get back to you.
