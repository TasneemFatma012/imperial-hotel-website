FAQ


Can I arrange airport transfers at the hotel?

Airport transfers from the hotel to the airport can be arranged through the Concierge Desk at your chosen hotel. Please note these transfers are chargeable.

Are all of the rooms in your hotels en-suite?

All of our rooms are en-suite. All bathrooms come equipped with a bath and a shower, except some rooms at the President, Tavistock and Royal National Hotels which contain a shower only.

Are safety deposit boxes available at all of your hotels?

Please contact Reception at your chosen hotel directly, for information relating to the safety deposit boxes at each hotel.

Are the bedrooms in all of your hotels air-conditioned?

We do not have air conditioning in any of our hotel bedrooms, except at our Morton Hotel.
